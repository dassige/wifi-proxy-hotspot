Exports everything you configure here as one JSON file: proxy profiles, routing rules and their settings (default, Proxied Lookups, leak guards), the internal bypass range, email alert settings, and device names, routing and blocks. It includes proxy and SMTP passwords in plain text, so store the downloaded file securely. The Wi-Fi SSID/passphrase are not included and must be reconfigured separately if restoring to a new box.
