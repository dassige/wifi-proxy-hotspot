Email Alerts {{ help_btn('help-alerts') }}

{{ help_dialog('help-alerts', HELP_ALERTS) }}

Emails you when a health check on the Overview page starts failing, and again when it recovers, plus one-off notices (box restarted, under-voltage, a proxy's exit IP changing). Nothing here ever changes routing.

These keep a flapping proxy from flooding your inbox: a problem only counts as recovered once it has stayed OK this long, and alerts arriving soon after an email are grouped into the next one. 0 turns either limit off.
